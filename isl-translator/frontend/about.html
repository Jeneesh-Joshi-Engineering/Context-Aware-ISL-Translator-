Why we built this

Access starts with a conversation.

At a railway counter or public service desk, asking for help should be straightforward. ISL Bridge connects Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing visitors with staff through two synchronized screens.

Visitors scan a permanent counter sign and use their camera to sign. Recognized words become readable sentences; staff reply by voice or typing.

Our current prototype

This academic demonstration recognizes three trained signs: Help, Ticket, and Train, with a No_Gesture idle class. It does not interpret arbitrary Indian Sign Language sentences. Hand tracking and recognition run locally; camera video is not sent to the server.
